CrossChat runs only on this PC. It has no server, no account and no telemetry.

It connects only to:
  - the chat platforms you set up (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, X, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Rumble), and
  - unless you turn them off in CrossChat's Settings, 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ for emote pictures.
    They get your Twitch channel's public id.
If you switch it on in CrossChat's Settings, it also asks GitHub once a day whether a newer CrossChat is out.

Your sign-ins (Twitch, Kick, Rumble, Discord) are kept on this PC, encrypted for your Windows account.
Replies to YouTube, X, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram are typed into those sites' own pages in your browser;
the CrossChat browser extension reads no logins.

Setup installs CrossChat for your Windows account only, in your AppData folder, with its own copy of
Python from python.org. It doesn't need administrator rights and changes nothing else on this PC,
except, if you agree, switching on OBS's WebSocket server so CrossChat can follow your stream.
