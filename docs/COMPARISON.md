## Where CrossChat falls short

- Social Stream Ninja reads more than 100 other sites. CrossChat reads these 8.
- Streamlabs Desktop has a TikTok integration, announced in April 2024 as a partnership with TikTok. CrossChat reads your TikTok LIVE page, so a TikTok redesign can break it until CrossChat is updated. ([source](https://support.streamlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/24730717904795-Streamlabs-Announces-Integration-With-TikTok))
- Restream and Streamlabs Multichat let you sign in from any browser. CrossChat has no account and no server by design: its window and phone companion open in a browser on your network, or away from home through Tailscale, and connect to your streaming PC.
- CrossChat's Rumble moderation has only been tested on the streamer's own message so far, not on another viewer. Its Linux version was tested with Google Chrome on Ubuntu only.
