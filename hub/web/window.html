            Connections

              TikTok, Facebook and Instagram chat comes from your own live page in Chrome or Edge, and Rumble replies go through Rumble’s chat window there. CrossChat opens them when you go live, and replies are typed there for you. Facebook comments can be deleted, and Rumble messages deleted or their senders muted, from a message’s menu here; moderate TikTok and Instagram on the platform itself.
